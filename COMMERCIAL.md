# Commercial licensing

SEO Audit Console is free to download, build, and run unmodified for personal use, evaluation, and education - no licence needed, no time limit. See [LICENSE](./LICENSE) for the exact terms.

Using it commercially - inside a business, for client work, or in anything that makes money - needs a commercial licence. This page is how you get one.

And there's a promise built into the licence itself: **every released version automatically becomes Apache 2.0 open source three years after its release.** You're licensing the current edge, not the idea forever.

## What a commercial licence doesn't cover

Modification beyond what's needed to build and run, redistribution (npm, marketplaces, bundling into your own product), and hosting it as a service each need separate written permission regardless of tier - see LICENSE section 3. If that's what you're after, the conversation is usually short and friendly: **hello@houtini.com**.
