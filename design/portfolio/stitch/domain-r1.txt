Design ONE desktop screen (1440 wide, light theme, a long scrolling page) for a local SEO console: the "Domain overview" page for one website, opened from a portfolio table. Keep the existing design system: warm monochrome, crisp 1px borders, mono numerals, pale pastel badges, one green accent, no gradients, no heavy shadows, no emoji. Every number must be a literal "[figure]" placeholder. Do not invent brands, logos or marketing copy. Spell out table headers in full words. No em dashes anywhere.

The site in view is an OWNED site, so the page shows measured Google Search Console data next to third-party estimates, and must always label which is which ("measured" vs "estimated").

Layout, top to bottom:

1. Breadcrumb "Domains / [domain]". Title "[domain]" with a country flag + code, a "GSC" badge, and a link "Open full property dashboard". Right side: "Data captured [figure]" and a Month | Year toggle.

2. Two side-by-side blocks:
   a) "Search (estimated)": a grid of 6 KPI tiles: Domain Rank (out of 100, third-party), Organic traffic (estimated), Organic keywords, Paid traffic (estimated), Referring domains, Backlinks. Each tile: label, big mono value, small change chip, and "vs [month]".
   b) "Search Console (measured)": a narrower strip with Clicks 28 days, Impressions 28 days, Pages with clicks. Each with change. A tiny caption "Measured by Google, all countries".
   Under both, a slim bar "Traffic share among [figure] chosen domains: [figure]%" with a small stacked horizontal bar of the domains in the group.

3. "AI search" section (the most important new section). Header with a platform segmented control "AI Overviews | ChatGPT (US)". Contents:
   - Three KPI tiles: Mentions, AI search volume, Cited pages, each with change vs last complete month.
   - A combo chart: monthly bars for Mentions with a line for AI search volume on a second axis, 15 months. The current month bar is drawn hollow (outlined only) with a label "to date".
   - A muted footnote slot: "AI Mode, Gemini and Perplexity are not measured by this data source." Do NOT show any 0-100 visibility score, gauge or grade word.
   - Below, a two-column area:
     Left: "Keywords: rank and citation" table. Columns: Keyword, AI Overview shown (yes / no), Our page cited (yes / no / unknown, where unknown is a grey pill), Organic rank, Impressions 28 days, Clicks 28 days. About 8 rows.
     Right: a quadrant scatter "Rank and citation" with four labelled quadrants: "Ranks and cited", "Ranks, not cited", "Cited, not ranking", "Neither". Bubbles sized by impressions.
   - Then two tables side by side: "Top cited pages" (Page, Mentions, Clicks 28 days, Crawl status as a small pill e.g. "200 indexable", "noindex", "301") and "Sources cited alongside" (Domain, Mentions, as horizontal bars). Caption under the second: "Sources, not competitors".

4. "Traffic trend": a wide line chart, 24 months. Organic traffic (estimated) as a solid line, Paid traffic (estimated) as a thin line, and Search Console clicks (measured) as a dashed line on its own right-hand axis. Legend states "estimated" and "measured" explicitly. Hollow last point for the partial month.

5. Two charts side by side: "Keyword positions" (stacked area over 24 months, bands: positions 1-3, 4-10, 11-20, 21-50, 51-100) and "Search results mix" (a donut: Organic, AI Overview references, Featured snippets, Local pack, with a legend showing counts and %).

6. "Countries": a compact table (Country, Organic traffic estimated, Keywords, Share) with inline share bars, top 8.

7. "Backlinks": a bar chart of new vs lost referring domains per month (paired bars, 12 months) with a line for total referring domains.

8. "Top pages" with tabs "By traffic (estimated)" and "By links". Show the By links tab: columns Page, Referring domains, Backlinks, Broken backlinks, Our crawl status (pill), Clicks 28 days. One row highlighted with a red-dot note "Linked page returns 404 in our crawl".

Calm, analyst-grade, dense but well spaced; cards with 1px borders and small uppercase section labels; right-aligned mono numerals.
